Recruitment Process:
1st Round:    Written Test:     Day 1

Assessment flow
1.    Logical Puzzle Round
o    All candidates will initially attend the Logical Puzzle assessment.
o    The assessment will comprise questions covering Logical Reasoning (Verbal and Non-Verbal), Geometrical Problems, Data Interpretation, Algebraic Problems, Mathematical Problem Modelling, Real-Life Aptitude Problems involving Time, Speed & Distance and Time & Work, Practical and Analytical Puzzles, Probability, Combinatorics, Statistics, Physics, and Physical Properties.
Candidates are advised to prepare across these areas to perform effectively in the assessment.
2.    Hardware Assessment
o    Immediately after the puzzle round, the Hardware MCQ question paper will be distributed to all candidates.
o    Based on their performance in the MCQ assessment, shortlisted candidates will be invited to proceed to the Subjective Technical Assessment round.
3.    Software Assessment
o    Candidates shortlisted based on the Logical Puzzle evaluation will proceed to the Technical discussion on Day 2.
4.    Hardware Interview Shortlisting
o    Candidates will be shortlisted for the Hardware interview primarily based on the Hardware assessment scores (Both Objective & Subjective).
o    Logical Puzzle scores will be considered only as a reference parameter.

What to Prepare for the Hardware Assessment
Candidates are advised to prepare the following topics for the Hardware Assessment:
•    Basic Electrical Components and Parameters: Voltage, Current, Power, Resistor, Inductor, and Capacitor
•    Fundamental Electrical Laws and Applications: Ohm's Law, Kirchhoff's Voltage Law (KVL), Kirchhoff's           Current Law (KCL), Superposition Theorem, and other basic circuit laws
•    Measuring Instruments: Ammeter, Voltmeter, and Multimeter
•    Transient and Steady-State Behavior of Inductors, Capacitors, and Associated Circuits
•    Circuit Analysis Techniques: Mesh Analysis and Nodal Analysis
•    Transformers: Working Principle, Types, and Applications
•    Electronic Devices and Applications: PN Diode, Zener Diode, Bipolar Junction Transistor (BJT), and Field Effect Transistor (FET)
•    Digital Electronics: Logic Gates, Multiplexers, Demultiplexers, and Flip-Flops
•    Microcontrollers and Microprocessors: Architectural Basics, Memory Interfacing, and Peripheral Interfacing
•    Data Converters: Analog-to-Digital Converters (ADC) and Digital-to-Analog Converters (DAC)
•    Operational Amplifiers (Op-Amps): Basic Concepts and Applications
•    Communication Protocols: I²C and SPI
•    Power Regulators: LDO Regulators and Buck Converters
•    Filters: Low-Pass, High-Pass, and Band-Pass Filters
•    Transmission Line Fundamentals
Candidates are encouraged to focus on the basic concepts, working principles, applications, and problem-solving related to the above topics.
